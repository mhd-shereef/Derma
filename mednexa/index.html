How it works

Three unhurried steps.

01

Connect

Tell us who you are — an individual, a wellness program, or a clinic. Diagnox meets you with a guided check-in; Derma imports your schedule and patient list without drama.

02

Understand

Diagnox turns answers and measures into readable screening insights. Derma turns visits, photos, and notes into one continuous patient story.

03

Act, with a human

Bring the summary to your doctor. Every Diagnox report says so plainly — software screens, physicians decide. Every Derma chart is signed by one.

Trust & security

Health data is intimate. We treat it that way.

Encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access so staff see only what their role needs, and full audit trails on patient data. Hosting and retention are agreed per clinic — ask us and we will put it in writing.

Encryption TLS in transit; AES-256 at rest. Private image storage with short-lived signed access.
Access control Doctor, nurse, receptionist, admin — each role sees only its scope. Every access is logged.
No silent AI Diagnox explains which answers shaped its insight, and always defers to a qualified doctor.
Your data, exportable Clinics can export their records. No lock-in, no surprises.
