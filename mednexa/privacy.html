Principles we commit to

  • Health data is processed only to provide the requested service, never sold.
  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256); private image storage with short-lived signed access.
  • Role-based access and immutable audit logs on patient data access.
  • Data residency and retention agreed per clinic and documented in the service agreement.
  • Individuals may export and request deletion of their Diagnox data, subject to legal retention duties which will be stated here.
